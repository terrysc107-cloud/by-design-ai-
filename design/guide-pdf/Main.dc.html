AI by Design

The Board
Method

Five steps that take AI from something you operate to a board that runs on a schedule and hands you a meeting you can act on.

01 Charter Who the seat is, not what you asked it this time
02 Floor A number the month can fall below, not a target
03 Run On a schedule, so it happens without you
04 Review Telling a useful run from a plausible one
05 Promote Autonomy earned in steps, and revocable
aixdesign.dev Every example in this guide is a real file from a board that is running.
