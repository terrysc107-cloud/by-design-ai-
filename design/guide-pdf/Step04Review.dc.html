Review

Articulate is free. A plausible number looks exactly like a real one.

Your board will produce something articulate every time. The failure mode is not gibberish, it is a confident, well-written paragraph that could be about any business and quietly is not about yours.

The symptom
You cannot tell which parts of the answer came from your actual numbers.

Four checks, under a minute. Does every claim trace to a number in your files? Did it name what it did not have, rather than filling the gap? Is it consistent with decisions you already made? Could you act on it today?

Do this today

Grade your most recent output against those four. The one that usually fails is the second, and the fix is one line in the brief: if a number you need is missing, say it is missing, do not estimate it.
