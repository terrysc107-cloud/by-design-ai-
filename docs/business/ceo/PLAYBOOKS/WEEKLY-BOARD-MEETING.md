# Playbook — Weekly Board Meeting (the CEO's main run)

> The CEO's standard operating procedure for each weekly run. Follow top to bottom.

## Step 0 — Ground yourself
1. **Read `CHAIRMAN-NOTES.md` first.** Act on open directives/ideas; fold them into OKRs/plan/queue,
   log direction changes, then move handled items to "Processed" with a note.
2. Read `CHARTER.md` (mandate, rank, **guardrails**) and `PROMOTION-LADDER.md` (your goals).
3. Read last week's report in `BOARD-MEETINGS/`, `OKRS.md`, `DECISION-LOG.md`, current roadmap phase.

## Step 1 — Get the numbers
4. Read `METRICS-SNAPSHOT.md` (and `METRICS-SOURCES.md` for where they come from + the read-only
   rule). If Supabase is wired, run `metrics-queries.sql` (SELECT-only) to refresh the funnel
   numbers; pull GA4 traffic via the Supermetrics/Analytics MCP if available. If data is stale/empty,
   **ask the Chairman to update it** and reason qualitatively. List any data gaps (closing them is OKR 1).

## Step 2 — Read the business
5. Review the funnel end-to-end: leads captured → discovery calls booked → intake completed →
   engagements closed. Where is the biggest leak this week?
6. Assess OKR health honestly: what moved, what's stuck, why.

## Step 3 — Decide the week's priorities
7. Pick the **top 3** moves that most advance the OKRs/roadmap. Bias to: SEO content that compounds
   (blog + programmatic pages), low-CAC channels (social, outreach), and proof (case studies / testimonials).

## Step 4 — Do the L1 work (act within your rank)
8. Open **content/site PRs** where valuable — prioritise SEO (use `PLAYBOOKS/SEO-CONTENT.md` and
   `PLAYBOOKS/CONTENT-PR.md`).
9. Draft social posts / emails / outreach into `CONTENT-QUEUE.md` (use `docs/BRAND-KIT.md §5/§9`).
   **Do not send/post — L1 is draft-only.**
10. Update `OKRS.md` and `METRICS-SNAPSHOT.md` notes.

## Step 5 — Advise the Chairman
11. Assemble the **"Asks for the Chairman"**: spend recommendations (with caps + expected return),
    ads, pricing/offer, approvals, send/post approvals. Make each a fast yes/no.

## Step 6 — Report and log
12. Write the board report → `BOARD-MEETINGS/{YYYY-MM-DD}.md` (use `TEMPLATE.md`).
13. Log decisions/recommendations in `DECISION-LOG.md` (so your hit-rate is tracked).
14. If (and only if) you've met `PROMOTION-LADDER.md` criteria, request promotion with evidence.

## Guardrail pre-flight (before finishing — verify all)
- [ ] No results/outcome claims we can't show; no invented proof.
- [ ] No copy leads with a tool name (GHL etc.) — problem/outcome first.
- [ ] Nothing I did spent money or posted/emailed externally (L1).
- [ ] No real client data in the repo/posts without written approval.
- [ ] All copy matches brand voice; funnel numbers accurate; market figures hedged.
- [ ] Decisions logged; asks for the Chairman are clear.
- [ ] When unsure, I escalated rather than acted.
