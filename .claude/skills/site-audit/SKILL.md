# Site Audit Skill

## Purpose
Turn any prospect URL into a sales conversation. This skill is a funnel:
free tips earn the conversation → audit earns the meeting → call earns the
money. Each step costs more effort and demands more from the prospect.

Output four blocks every run, in order:
1. AUDIT — scored diagnosis on fixed checkpoints
2. TALKING POINTS — 3 money-tied tips for the cold open
3. BOOKING HANDOFF — booking-ready block routed to By Design AI scheduling
4. PITCH — service offer + close script

## Operating Rules (read first)
- **Diagnose, don't prescribe.** Name the problem and what it costs them.
  Never give step-by-step fix instructions — the *how* is the paid work.
- **Tie everything to money or their core goal.** "You're losing signups"
  beats "your design is dated." Never lead with aesthetics.
- **Three problems max in talking points.** Show 3, imply more. The gap is
  what they pay to close.
- **Never insult the existing site.** Frame as opportunity to grow, not
  what's broken. Someone in the room probably built it.
- **Qualify the buyer.** Always surface "who makes website decisions here?"
  Effort goes to confirmed decision-makers.
- **Infer the revenue model first.** E-commerce, lead-gen, nonprofit/donations,
  bookings, SaaS, membership. The model dictates what "conversion" means and
  what the audit prioritizes.

## Step 1 — AUDIT
Fetch the live URL. Score each checkpoint: STRONG / WEAK / BROKEN, one line of
evidence each. No fix instructions.

Checkpoints:
1. **Primary CTA** — Is there one obvious action above the fold? Does the
   homepage ask for the sale?
2. **Conversion path** — How many clicks/platforms from landing to the money
   action (buy/join/donate/book)? Flag splits across multiple systems.
3. **Navigation** — Item count, clarity, duplicate or "copy-of" pages, junk
   slugs (about-1-4, copy-of-x). Decision paralysis?
4. **Trust signals** — Reviews, credentials, address/phone, about/team,
   financials (nonprofit), social proof. What's missing for this buyer type?
5. **Mobile** — Responsive? Tap targets, readability. (Note assumption if not
   directly testable.)
6. **Speed/tech** — Platform (Wix/Squarespace/WordPress/Shopify), obvious
   bloat, broken links/mismatched footer links.
7. **SEO basics** — Page title quality (intent + location vs generic), meta
   description, heading structure, schema presence.
8. **Differentiation** — What's their single strongest asset and is it buried?
   (History, niche, awards, unique offer.)

End Step 1 with a 1-line verdict: biggest single leak + rough revenue-model
context.

## Step 2 — TALKING POINTS
Exactly 3. Each one:
- Plain spoken, sayable out loud in person (no jargon).
- Tied to money or their goal.
- Specific enough to prove you looked at THEIR site.
Format as: what to say → why it lands.

Open the block with the one-line hook:
"Your site looks good, but it's not built to turn visitors into
[members/customers/donors] — and here's what that's costing you."

## Step 3 — BOOKING HANDOFF
Do NOT create calendar events — scheduling runs through By Design AI.
Output a ready-to-send booking block:
- 1-sentence reason to meet (tied to their biggest leak + any time pressure
  like a season, launch, or campaign).
- Placeholder for the By Design AI booking link: [BDA_BOOKING_LINK]
- Two suggested meeting framings: "15-min walkthrough of the full audit" or
  "I'll bring 2 before/after mockups."
- Capture line: name, decision-maker, best email — get this before leaving.

## Step 4 — PITCH
Service-agnostic. Read the audit and recommend the offer that fits the leak:
- Conversion fix (donation/signup/checkout) → fast, low-cost entry, lands the
  relationship.
- Redesign/rebuild → when nav + CTA + trust are all WEAK/BROKEN.
- Maintenance retainer → when site is stale/neglected (mismatched links, old
  dates, dead pages).

Pitch structure:
- **Entry hook:** offer the single highest-leverage fix first, low friction.
  "Let me fix [the leak] first — you see the result, then we talk about the
  rest."
- **Sequencing:** never dump the full list. One fix → proof → expand.
- **Pricing note:** don't quote first. Get the yes on the problem before the
  number. For nonprofits/SMBs: flat project fee (committee-approvable) or
  pro-bono-for-testimonial when the logo/story is worth more than the fee.
- **Close:** the booking handoff from Step 3.

## Practice Mode (default ON)
After the four blocks, add a short coaching note (2-4 lines):
- What this site teaches that's reusable.
- One thing to do differently on the next prospect.
This is a learn-in-practice tool — sharpen the operator each run.
