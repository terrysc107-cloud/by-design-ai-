# AI by Design Brand + Editing Specification

## Brand source

Derived from `https://aixdesign.dev/guide` and its deployed stylesheet on 2026-09-04. Re-check the live site before major brand-system changes.

## Identity

- Brand: **AI by Design** / `aixdesign`
- Positioning: practical AI business coaching, consulting, automation, and operating systems
- Core line: **Stop learning about AI. Start running on it.**
- Default lead magnet: **The Board Method**
- Guide promise: five steps that take AI from something you operate to a board that reads real numbers on a schedule and hands you a meeting you can act on
- Guide URL: `https://aixdesign.dev/guide`

## Color tokens

- Espresso background: `#1E1B17`
- Near-black alternate: `#23201B`
- AI by Design gold: `#C9A84C`
- Bright gold highlight: `#F0D080`
- Deep gold accent: `#B8902A`
- Primary text: `#FFFFFF`
- Muted text: use warm white at roughly 65–75% opacity
- Hairlines/panels: gold or white at 10–25% opacity

Use gold as a precision accent, not a flood fill. The screen recording should retain authentic application colors.

## Typography

The guide uses **Geist Sans** via `--font-geist-sans`, with system sans-serif fallback.

Recommended 1080×1920 master sizes:
- Hook: 78–92 px, ExtraBold, 0.95–1.0 line height
- Item name: 64–78 px, Bold/ExtraBold
- Ordinal chip: 30–38 px, Bold, letter-spaced
- Benefit bullets: 38–48 px, Medium/Semibold
- Burned captions: 52–64 px, Bold/ExtraBold
- CTA headline: 68–84 px, ExtraBold
- URL/keyword: 44–56 px, Bold
- Small brand/footer: 26–32 px, Medium

Actual sizes may shift with copy length, but mobile legibility wins. Never shrink a crowded layout below useful reading size; shorten the copy.

## Frame grammar

### Hook frame

- Espresso base or tightly cropped proof footage.
- Small gold eyebrow: `FIVE THINGS` or topic category.
- Large off-white hook, with one key phrase in gold.
- Fast proof montage may run under or beside the text.

### Item frame

- Gold ordinal chip at upper-left of the text zone.
- Item name directly below or beside it.
- Screen recording fills the largest region.
- Benefit bullets use gold four-point sparkles (`✦`) or a custom four-point SVG, aligned consistently.
- Use a thin gold border or corner bracket around the active proof region only when it improves focus.

### Screen recording

- Record cleanly at 1440p or above when possible.
- Increase browser/editor zoom before capture.
- Crop around the relevant control and result.
- Hide bookmarks, personal profile images, unrelated tabs, notifications, secrets, and customer information.
- Use gold cursor rings/callouts sparingly.
- If the result is text-heavy, show a close crop and highlight one line rather than the entire page.

### CTA frame

- Distinct visual scene from the list body.
- Espresso background plus real Board Method guide mockup or a recording of the live landing page.
- Headline: outcome of the guide, not merely “link in bio.”
- Gold keyword or gold URL.
- AI by Design wordmark/footer.
- Hold static legibility for at least 2.0 seconds after the last spoken phrase.

## Captions

- Phrase-level captions, center-aligned or left-aligned to match the frame grid.
- White/off-white base, gold emphasis on one semantic unit.
- A low-opacity espresso rounded rectangle is permitted over complex footage.
- Keep captions away from the item title; do not duplicate identical text in two competing zones.

## Audio

- Voice is primary and dry/clear.
- Background track: modern minimal electronic pulse, no lyrics, approximately 10–16 dB below voice.
- Light click/tap for bullet reveals and a restrained whoosh for item changes.
- Duck music further under the CTA.
- Target platform-safe loudness around -14 LUFS integrated and peaks below -1 dBTP.

## Export

- 1080×1920, H.264 High Profile, 30 fps.
- AAC, 48 kHz, 192–320 kbps.
- Recommended video bitrate 12–20 Mbps.
- Verify exact duration, audio presence, first-frame readability, representative item frames, and final CTA hold before delivery.
