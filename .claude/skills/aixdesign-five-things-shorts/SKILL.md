# AI by Design — Five Things Short

## Purpose

Create 60–80 second vertical videos that teach exactly five useful tools, moves, mistakes, workflows, prompts, or principles. Match the reference video's **content mechanics**—fast hook, one item at a time, proof-oriented screen recordings, large kinetic captions, and a distinct CTA ending—while using AI by Design's own identity rather than copying the source creator's branding.

This skill must produce a publishable production packet, not only five ideas.

## Inputs

Use available context without asking unless the missing input changes the deliverable:

- Topic or audience problem
- Five items, or permission to research/select them
- Destination platform(s): default YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels + TikTok
- CTA asset: default free Board Method guide at `https://aixdesign.dev/guide`
- Available screen-recording environment/assets

If the user supplies links or makes current product claims, verify them before scripting. Never invent features, integrations, metrics, pricing, or availability.

## Content Architecture

### 1. Hook: 0:00–0:04

Use one direct sentence:

> If you [do/use/want] **X**, these are the five **Y** you need [right now/to know].

Rules:
- Name the audience or job immediately.
- Promise exactly five items.
- No greeting, biography, setup, or logo animation.
- Show the most visually interesting proof behind the hook.

### 2. Five-item body: roughly 9–12 seconds each

Each item follows the same compact unit:

1. **Ordinal + name:** “First: [item].”
2. **Outcome:** what it lets the viewer accomplish.
3. **Proof:** show the actual screen action or result.
4. **Friction removed:** one plain-language contrast such as “No tab switching. No copy-pasting.”

Do not give all five equal explanation if one is obvious. Protect pace. Every sentence must either name the item, explain the payoff, demonstrate proof, or remove an objection.

Recommended timing:
- Item 1: 0:04–0:15
- Item 2: 0:15–0:26
- Item 3: 0:26–0:37
- Item 4: 0:37–0:49
- Item 5: 0:49–1:01
- Recap/bridge: 1:01–1:06
- CTA: 1:06–1:18

### 3. Recap bridge

Use one payoff line, not a full repeated list:

> Five tools. One operating system. Less switching, more finished work.

Adapt the nouns to the topic. Keep it under seven seconds.

### 4. CTA ending

Default AI by Design CTA:

> If you want AI to run standing work instead of waiting for you to prompt it, get the free Board Method guide at **aixdesign.dev/guide**.

Optional comment CTA—use only when comment-to-DM or manual fulfillment is actually configured:

> Comment **BOARD** and I’ll send you the free Board Method guide. Follow **AI by Design** so the message can reach you.

CTA rules:
- One offer, one keyword, one destination.
- Connect the resource to the lesson; do not bolt on a generic promotion.
- Show the real guide mockup or landing page.
- Hold the final URL/keyword card for at least 2.0 seconds.
- Never claim “the system only sends it to followers” unless the platform automation truly requires that behavior.

## Visual System

Read `references/brand-and-editing-spec.md` before producing assets.

Core composition:
- 9:16, 1080×1920 master, 30 fps.
- Screen recording is the evidence, not wallpaper. Crop tightly around the exact click, prompt, result, chart, or before/after.
- Use a two-zone layout when needed: proof recording in the upper or central 55–70%; headline and bullets in the remaining safe area.
- Use full-height screen proof when the interface itself is legible; add zooms/callouts rather than showing an entire unreadable desktop.
- Keep important content inside a 120 px side safe margin, 180 px top margin, and 300 px bottom margin to avoid platform UI.

### Five-item title/bullet treatment

For every item, show:
- A small gold ordinal chip: `01`, `02`, `03`, `04`, `05`
- A large 2–5 word item name
- Up to three short benefit bullets, revealed as spoken

Bullet style:
- Gold four-point sparkle or short gold rule; never default round bullets.
- 3–7 words per bullet.
- Sentence case; no periods unless a complete sentence.
- Active, concrete wording.
- Reveal one at a time with 120–180 ms stagger.

### Captions

- Use burned-in captions synchronized phrase by phrase, 2–6 words per phrase.
- Default typeface: Geist Sans, with Inter or SF Pro as fallback.
- Main captions: ExtraBold/Bold, white or warm off-white.
- Highlight only the key noun/verb in AI by Design gold.
- Use subtle dark shadow or a translucent espresso plate only when footage reduces contrast.
- Avoid karaoke coloring every word, outlined meme captions, excessive bounce, all-caps paragraphs, and tiny subtitles.
- Maximum two lines. Keep each line visually balanced.

### Motion and transitions

- Hard cuts dominate.
- Use 4–8% digital push-ins to direct attention.
- Use quick masked slides or scale-ins for each ordinal/title; 180–280 ms.
- Cursor/click emphasis: gold ring or soft pulse.
- Change visual state every 1.5–3 seconds through crop, zoom, callout, bullet reveal, or result change.
- Avoid glitch packs, spinning logos, 3D transitions, and decorative motion that competes with proof.

## Required Production Packet

Return these sections:

1. **Angle** — audience, problem, promise, and why these five belong together.
2. **Verified five** — item, current claim, source or proof status.
3. **Voiceover script** — 60–80 seconds, natural spoken language.
4. **Timecoded shot list** — timestamps, exact screen action, crop/zoom, overlay copy, transition.
5. **On-screen copy** — hook, each item title, bullets, recap, CTA card.
6. **Capture checklist** — exact pages/apps/states to record; hide private data, API keys, inboxes, notifications, customer data, and unrelated tabs.
7. **Edit spec** — dimensions, type sizes, colors, caption behavior, music/SFX, export.
8. **Platform package** — title, concise caption, CTA, and 3–5 relevant hashtags per platform.
9. **QA results** — verify runtime, five-item count, spelling, claims, safe zones, screen legibility, CTA destination, and final hold.

## Voice

AI by Design sounds like an experienced operator:
- Outcome-led, practical, specific, calm, and direct.
- Translate tools into business behavior.
- Prefer “what this lets you do” over feature lists.
- No breathless hype, “game changer,” “revolutionary,” fake scarcity, or generic AI clichés.
- Brand line when useful: **Stop learning about AI. Start running on it.**

## Quality Gate

Reject and revise if any condition fails:
- The hook does not identify the audience/job and promise five items by second 4.
- There are not exactly five clearly numbered items.
- Any item lacks visible proof or a planned proof shot.
- The screen recording is too small to understand on a phone.
- More than three bullets appear at once.
- Captions exceed two lines or enter platform UI zones.
- The CTA is unrelated, unverified, or has multiple competing actions.
- A current product claim has no verification.
- The final guide URL or keyword is not held long enough to read.

When rendering is requested, do not stop at the packet: capture or gather the approved recordings, build the timeline, export the MP4, inspect representative frames plus audio/runtime, and deliver the verified file.
